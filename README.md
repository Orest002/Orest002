## `> spotlight` — Vision Voice Assist 🕶️

<table>
<tr>
<td width="60%" valign="top">

**The problem.** For people who are blind or visually impaired, the everyday world is full of invisible information — what's in front of them, what a sign says, who just walked in.

**The idea.** Glasses that turn the visual world into natural spoken language, in real time.

- 👁️ **Real-time scene understanding** — a wearable camera + AI vision pipeline describes the surroundings out loud
- 🗣️ **Natural voice interaction** — ask about what's in front of you, get a spoken answer
- 🔊 **Hands-free, eyes-free** — worn, not held; no screen needed
- 🧠 **On-device + cloud hybrid** — built on a Raspberry Pi 5
